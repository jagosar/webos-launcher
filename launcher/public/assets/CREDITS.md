## Backgrounds

Cropped to 16:9 by Lightkeeper from the museums' and the Library of Congress's own images:

- `southampton.jpg`: James McNeill Whistler, *Nocturne: Blue and Gold—Southampton Water*, 1872. Art Institute of Chicago, public domain. https://www.artic.edu/artworks/56905
- `penobscot.jpg`: Fitz Henry Lane, *Lumber Schooners at Evening on Penobscot Bay*, 1863. National Gallery of Art, Washington, CC0. https://www.nga.gov/artworks/57611-lumber-schooners-evening-penobscot-bay
- `northcape.jpg`: Peder Balke, *The North Cape by Moonlight*, 1848. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, CC0. https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/441379
- `monk.jpg`: Caspar David Friedrich, *The Monk by the Sea*, 1808-1810. Alte Nationalgalerie, Berlin, public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Caspar_David_Friedrich_-_Der_M%C3%B6nch_am_Meer_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg
- `entrance.jpg`: James McNeill Whistler, *Nocturne in Black and Gold: Entrance to Southampton Water*, c. 1875-1876. Freer Gallery of Art, Smithsonian, CC0. https://asia-archive.si.edu/object/F1897.21a-b/
- `chinasea.jpg`: James Horsburgh, *China Sea, Sheet I*, 1806. Library of Congress, public domain; shown in negative. https://www.loc.gov/item/84696453/
- `night.jpg`, `dawn.jpg`: *Night at sea* and *Dawn at sea*, gradients made for Lightkeeper, MIT licence (`backgrounds/LICENSE-lightkeeper.txt`).
